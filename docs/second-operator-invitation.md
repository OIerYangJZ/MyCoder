# Would you spend an hour breaking something for me?

I am asking for **one hour of your time, on your own computer**, and I am not
allowed to help you during it. That sounds like a strange request, so here is
exactly what it is.

## What you would be doing

There is a command-line program I have been building. You would:

1. install it, from a published package;
2. set it up to talk to an AI model provider, using only the written instructions
   that ship with it;
3. point it at some code of your own — anything, a side project, a script, a
   repository I have never seen;
4. ask it to do one thing you actually wanted done;
5. and if it changes something you did not want changed, get that change back.

That is the whole hour. If you finish in twenty minutes, the hour is over.

## What I will not tell you

**What the program does, or how.** Not before, not during. If I explain it, you
arrive with my mental model instead of your own, and your hour stops being worth
anything to me — I would have to throw the result away. So the answer to "wait,
what is this thing?" is: that is the part I need you to find out.

## What I will not do

**Answer questions. Fix your machine. Suggest what to try. Watch over your
shoulder and steer.** I will not prepare anything on your computer beforehand.

This is not me being difficult. Everything about this program has been used by
exactly one person — me, the person who wrote it — and every convenience I hand
you during the hour is a piece of evidence I have to subtract from the result. If
you get stuck and cannot continue, **that is the single most valuable outcome of
the exercise** and I would rather have it than a smooth run.

## What I actually want out of it

Not whether you succeed. What I want is:

- every moment you had to stop and look something up, or guess;
- every moment you expected it to do one thing and it did another — including the
  times nothing broke and it just was not what you thought;
- every question you wanted to ask me and could not.

There is a blank sheet for this: `docs/second-operator-recording-sheet.md`. It is
three tables and you fill them in yourself, so I do not need to be in the room at
all. Send it back however you like. If your notes are three words per line, that
is fine — I would rather have rough notes than a report.

## What you need, and what it costs you

A computer with Node 22.18 or newer and a network connection. Some code of your
own you do not mind an AI editing. **Do not prepare anything else** — an
unprepared machine is the measurement.

The model provider will charge you a few cents at most, and I will cover it if you
want. You do not need to have used a tool like this before; if you have not, your
hour is worth more to me, not less. You do not need to know the language it is
written in, and please do not read its source first.

## What happens afterwards

Your notes go into a public repository as a findings table, dated. Your name goes
in only if you want it to. Anything you say about the product being confusing
stays in, verbatim, including the parts that make me look bad — that is the
record I am trying to create, and a version of it with the embarrassing lines
removed would be worth nothing.

If you are willing: any hour, any week. Nothing needs scheduling in advance and
nothing needs to be ready on my side.
