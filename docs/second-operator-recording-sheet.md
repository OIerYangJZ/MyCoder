# Recording sheet — fill this in yourself

For the hour described in `docs/second-operator-invitation.md`. Fill it in as you
go, not afterwards; the things worth having are the ones you forget within ten
minutes of solving them.

**Rough notes are the right format.** Three words in a cell beats a paragraph you
did not write. Nothing here is a test of you.

**An empty sheet is not a good outcome.** If you got through the hour with nothing
to write down, please say so explicitly at the bottom, because "nothing happened"
and "I did not write it down" have to be tellable apart.
